### CLIM-000055: Absolute radiometric closure of Earth’s energy imbalance
- **Subfield:** Climate-model inference and observing infrastructure
- **Open:** Sustain radiation measurements accurate and continuous enough to independently resolve changes in planetary energy imbalance. Close calibration, sampling, and radiance-to-flux errors against heat storage.
- **Status:** A decades-old metrology challenge; CLARREO established an SI-traceable approach, while heat inventories provide complementary constraints.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** Measures whether planetary heat accumulation is accelerating.
- **Key works:** Wielicki et al. (2013), [“Achieving Climate Change Absolute Accuracy in Orbit”](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-12-00149.1).
